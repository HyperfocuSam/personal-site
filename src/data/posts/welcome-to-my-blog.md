# Welcome to My Blog

Welcome to this new space where I'll share insights, learnings, and thoughts on AI adoption, workshops, and the human side of technology.

## Why Start a Blog?

After conducting 200+ one-on-one AI coaching sessions and dozens of corporate workshops, I've accumulated a wealth of observations about how people interact with AI, what works, and what doesn't.

This blog is my way of sharing those insights more broadly.

## What to Expect

You'll find posts covering:

- **Workshop Learnings** - Patterns I observe when training teams on AI
- **AI Insights** - Practical tips and perspectives on using AI effectively
- **Case Studies** - Stories from real implementations (anonymized, of course)
- **Industry Trends** - My take on where AI is heading
- **Personal Reflections** - The human side of this technological revolution
