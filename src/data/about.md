# Intro

I grew up with ADHD but didn't know it until my mid-20s. As a history graduate working as an executive assistant, I spent years feeling like I was constantly fighting my own brain—distracted, scattered, wondering if I'd ever find my path.

That changed when I found AI and automation. What started as tools to manage my own challenges became something bigger: a realization that the people who struggle most with traditional ways of working might be the ones who benefit most from new ones.

---

# The Discovery

AI didn't just help me work better. It helped me think differently about work itself.

I started building systems—not just for efficiency, but for clarity. Frameworks for turning chaos into structure. Ways to make the overwhelming feel manageable. And I noticed: the things that helped my ADHD brain also helped everyone else.

That's when the mission became clear. Not teaching AI. Helping people discover what's possible when they have the right tools and the right mindset.

---

# The Path

My career took me from Executive Assistant roles at **FAO Schwarz** and **Sharper Image** to becoming the **AI Product Manager and AI Team Lead at RENPHO**. There, I got to build an AI division from scratch—not just implementing tools, but changing how people thought about their work.

I learned something important: technology adoption isn't a training problem. It's a psychology problem. The tools are the easy part. The hard part is helping people feel safe enough to try something new, and confident enough to keep going.

---

# What I Believe

**Humans are the point.** AI is a tool. A powerful one, but still a tool. The goal isn't to make people more efficient—it's to free them to do work that matters.

**Start with the smallest step.** Grand transformations fail. Small wins compound. I look for the "Goldilocks Zone"—ambitious enough to matter, achievable enough to try.

**Psychological safety comes first.** People don't adopt what they fear. Before teaching skills, I create space where questions are welcome and mistakes are expected.

**Frameworks beat features.** Tools change. Thinking patterns endure. I teach approaches that work regardless of which AI is trending this month.

---

# Where I Work Now

In 2024, I left the corporate path to focus fully on helping others navigate theirs. You never know what happens next in life.

**Adaptig** — Co-founded with partners who share the belief that AI training should transform people, not just inform them. Our trainer network spans 20+ countries, helping organizations like Mattel, Toyota, and YPO go from unsure to unstoppable.

I also helped build **DotAI**, Hong Kong's practical AI training community, contributing to corporate training across 70+ organizations.

**Loopem** — An experiment in bringing practical AI to mainland China.

# The Bigger Picture

What motivates me isn't the technology. It's the moment when someone goes from "AI isn't for me" to "wait, I could use this for..."

We're living through something significant. Not just a technological shift, but a chance to rethink how we work, create, and solve problems. I believe the people who will thrive aren't those who master every tool—they're the ones who learn to think alongside AI while staying human.

The Fourth Industrial Revolution is also a Renaissance. And like any renaissance, it belongs to those who show up.
