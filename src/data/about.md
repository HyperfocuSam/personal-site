# Intro

I believe humans are the most important factor as we enter the AI era. My goal is to help people and organizations unlock their potential with AI while staying true to their unique strengths and values.

---

# My Journey

I grew up with ADHD but was only diagnosed in my mid-20s. As a history graduate working as an executive assistant, I struggled with the challenges ADHD brought to my daily life. At times, I felt directionless and frustrated, wondering if I would ever find my path.

That changed when I discovered AI and automation. What started as a tool for managing my own challenges became a mission to help others.

---

# From EA to AI Specialist

My career took me from Executive Assistant roles at international brands like **FAO Schwarz** and **Sharper Image** to becoming the **AI Product Manager and AI Team Lead at RENPHO**. There, I led strategic AI transformation projects, establishing an AI division and building tools that enhanced operational efficiency.

During this journey, I realized my true passion: helping others navigate their own AI journeys.

---

# Building the Future

In 2024, I took the leap to focus fully on AI training and consulting:

**Adaptig** - Co-founded with partners to create a global AI workshop platform. Our trainer network spans North America, Latin America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Serving clients like Mattel, Toyota, YPO, and more.

**Loopem** - Exploring AI product opportunities in the mainland China market.
