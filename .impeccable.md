## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary:** Corporate decision-makers (L&D heads, CHROs, innovation leads) evaluating Sam for AI training engagements. Arrive from LinkedIn, referrals, or search. Scanning for credibility + fit in under 60 seconds.
- **Secondary:** Individuals seeking 1-1 AI coaching. Often already follow Sam on social (Threads, LinkedIn). Want to understand services and book.
- **Tertiary:** Media, event organizers, and potential partners checking credentials (media kit, speaking page).
- **Context:** Desktop-first browsing during work hours, but mobile for social-referred traffic. Bilingual EN/TC audience (Hong Kong base, international reach).
- **Job to be done:** "Can I trust this person to train my team on AI?" → evidence through case studies, testimonials, and professional presentation.

### Brand Personality
- **3 words:** Friendly, Energetic, Modern
- **Voice:** First-person, practitioner tone. Speaks from experience, not theory. Uses specifics (named tools, real clients, actual results) over vague claims.
- **Emotional goals:** Visitors should feel: "This person is sharp, approachable, and clearly knows their stuff. Not corporate, not hype — real."
- **Design metaphor:** "Warm study at dusk" — intellectual warmth meets contemporary energy. Think: a well-designed co-working space, not a law firm or a startup garage.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Clean editorial, warm palette, breathing whitespace. Typography-driven hierarchy with serif headings (DM Serif Display) and clean sans body (Inter).
- **Primary reference:** Ali Abdaal — warm tones, editorial layout, generous spacing, serif + sans pairing, photography as personality signal.
- **Color system:** Warm neutral surfaces (#f6f4f0 base), navy ink (#0f1729), amber accent (#c8965a). No cold grays. Shadows are navy-tinted, not black.
- **Theme:** Light mode only. The warmth comes from cream surfaces and amber accents, not dark mode.
- **Typography:** Serif headings for editorial authority, sans body for readability. Bilingual support (Noto Sans TC for Chinese content).
- **Imagery:** Real photos of Sam (no stock). Grayscale client logos. Case study images are authentic.

### Anti-References (What This Must NOT Look Like)
- **No generic SaaS:** No gradient hero blobs, no "trusted by 10,000+" counters, no floating chat widgets, no product landing page energy.
- **No corporate consultancy:** No stock photography, no navy-and-white blandness, no "Our Methodology" funnels, not McKinsey-lite.
- **No AI hype:** No glowing neural networks, no "powered by AI" badges, no dark mode with neon accents, no crypto/Web3 aesthetic. The `#6adcfa` cyan currently on the skills page is exactly this anti-pattern.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth over polish.** Every surface should feel inviting, not sterile. Cream > white. Amber > blue. Serif > geometric sans for headings.
2. **Evidence over claims.** Design should showcase real work (case studies, testimonials, blog posts) not marketing superlatives. Let the content prove credibility.
3. **Breathing room.** Generous spacing, constrained content width (65ch for reading), clear visual hierarchy. If it feels cramped, it's wrong.
4. **One palette, no exceptions.** Every color must trace back to `_vars.scss` tokens. Hard-coded hex values are bugs. Off-palette colors (like `#6adcfa`) are design debt.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Keyboard navigable. Semantic HTML. Not as an afterthought — as a baseline.

### Design Token Sources
- **Colors:** `src/static/css/libs/_vars.scss` → `$palette` map
- **Typography:** `$font` map in same file + `src/static/css/base/_typography.scss`
- **Spacing:** `$size` map (element-height, element-margin, section-spacing, content widths)
- **Shadows:** `$shadow` map (sm/md/lg)
- **Radii:** `$radius` map (sm/md/lg/xl/full)
- **Durations:** `$duration` map (menu, transition)
