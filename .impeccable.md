## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary:** Corporate decision-makers (L&D heads, CHROs, innovation leads) evaluating Sam for AI training engagements. Arrive from LinkedIn, referrals, or search. Scanning for credibility + fit in under 60 seconds.
- **Secondary:** Individuals seeking 1-1 AI coaching. Often already follow Sam on social (Threads, LinkedIn). Want to understand services and book.
- **Tertiary:** Media, event organizers, and potential partners checking credentials (media kit, speaking page).
- **Context:** Desktop-first browsing during work hours, but mobile for social-referred traffic. Bilingual EN/TC audience (Hong Kong base, international reach).
- **Job to be done:** "Can I trust this person to train my team on AI?" → evidence through case studies, testimonials, and professional presentation.

### Brand Personality
- **3 words:** Friendly, Energetic, Modern
- **Voice:** First-person, practitioner tone. Speaks from experience, not theory. Uses specifics (named tools, real clients, actual results) over vague claims.
- **Emotional goals:** Visitors should feel: "This person is sharp, approachable, and clearly knows their stuff. Not corporate, not hype — real."
- **Design metaphor:** "Warm study at dusk" — intellectual warmth meets contemporary energy. Think: a well-designed co-working space, not a law firm or a startup garage.
