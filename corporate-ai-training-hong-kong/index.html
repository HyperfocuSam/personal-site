Why Most Corporate AI Training Fails

Most corporate AI training fails because it teaches tools, not habits. Your team attends a workshop, tries ChatGPT for a week, then goes back to doing things the old way. The training checked a box. It didn’t change behavior.

After training 10,000+ professionals across 70+ organizations, the pattern is clear: one-off sessions produce awareness, not adoption. People forget 70% of new information within 24 hours. The fix isn’t better content — it’s a different structure.

My approach starts from your team’s actual workflows — the emails they write, the reports they build, the decisions they make. We redesign those workflows with AI integrated at each step. Behavior change sticks because it’s tied to real work, not hypothetical demos.

The Pioneer Program model works because it’s built around change management principles: workflow-first exercises, weekly accountability, and internal champions who sustain adoption after the trainer leaves.

Hong Kong Government AI Training Support

The 2026-27 Budget earmarked HK$50 million for AI literacy and established a Committee on AI+ chaired by the Financial Secretary. While most measures target public awareness rather than corporate training, several funding paths are available:

  • NITTP — The New Industrialisation and Technology Training Programme, formerly RTTP. Since 1 August 2025 it matches 1:1 rather than 2:1 — half your course fee — capped at HK$250,000 per company per financial year, one course per trainee per year. The course must be registered with the VTC first.
  • BUD Fund — Up to HK$7 million cumulative per company, with fast-track Easy BUD applications capped at HK$150,000 each. From 15 June 2026 it carries targeted support for projects involving AI elements. Training can sit inside a funded project; a standalone workshop is not eligible on its own.
  • ERB — The Employees Retraining Board is adding AI application courses to its curriculum for employed workers.

The Technology Voucher Programme is often mentioned in this context and has been closed to new applications since 31 December 2024.

Don’t wait for government programs to be fully rolled out. Companies that invest in structured AI training now will have a 12-18 month head start.
